Lead story · Ukraine

Ukraine strikes Kerch Bridge for a second day

Ukraine hit the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea again on Tuesday, in what appears to be the largest Ukrainian drone attack of the war. Russia says it shot down 1,441 drones.

Unverified video appears to show the bridge’s road arch span collapsed into the Kerch Strait. President Zelenskyy confirmed the strike, calling it retaliation for Russian attacks on Ukrainian infrastructure. Russia pounded Kyiv’s bridges in reply.

Middle East · Bahrain

Iran fires drones at US radar base in Bahrain

Iran fired drones at a US radar system at Sheikh Isa base; Bahrain said it intercepted the attack, with no casualties or damage. US officials told CNN Iran is developing plans to target senior Trump officials with a “dirty bomb” or chemical weapon in Washington — unconfirmed.

Asia · Diplomacy

Trump in Manila; Xi, Trump and Putin to meet in Shenzhen

President Trump arrived in Manila for the ASEAN summit, then travels to China. China confirmed Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin will meet in Shenzhen on Thursday 19 November on the sidelines of APEC.

Europe · Security

Russian jets violate Japanese airspace; Estonia to shut border

Japan scrambled fighter jets and “strongly” protested after two suspected Russian aircraft violated its airspace early Tuesday. Estonia will close its border crossings with Russia from 1 December, citing a foiled Russian plot to bomb a drone plant.

Britain · West Bank

UK sanctions Israeli settlement builders

The UK announced sanctions on eight Israeli construction companies building settlements in the occupied West Bank, and on an activist group blocking aid to Gaza.
